# PRODUCT.md

## Overview
NovaStore is a full-stack e-commerce platform and executive operations system. It comprises a customer storefront (React 19) for browsing, purchasing, and managing orders, and an administrative dashboard (Laravel 11 / Blade / Filament) for catalog management, order fulfillment, multi-department operations, and business governance.

## Platform
web

## Target Users
1. **End Customers / Shoppers:** Fast, frictionless catalog navigation, clear transparent pricing, responsive cart and checkout, reliable order tracking.
2. **Operations & Executive Team:** Store administrators, department leads (Logistics, Ops, Finance, CSKH), and executive leadership needing clean, reliable data, fast review/approvals, and precise operational workflows.

## Primary Workflows
- Storefront: Catalog discovery, search, filtering, product detail viewing, cart management, checkout, account history.
- Dashboard: Orders management, inventory & PO tracking, customer support, category/product management, executive metrics and expenditure approvals.

## Core Values & Tone
- **Storefront:** Confident, clean, premium e-commerce with intentional whitespace, crisp typography, and zero gimmicks.
- **Dashboard:** High-density, calm, structured, utilitarian clarity with fast keyboard/mouse workflows and dependable visual hierarchy.
